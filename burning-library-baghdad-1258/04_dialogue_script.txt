## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: You guard the largest library in the world — every book the Islamic Golden Age ever produced. The Mongols are coming for it. You would not want to be in Baghdad, 1258.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: Your caliph refused to surrender. Now a hundred and fifty thousand cavalry surround your walls — walls built for floods, not sieges.

## Clip 03 — Ending
NARRATOR: You die because one caliph refused to negotiate — and four hundred years of human discovery drowns with you in a single week.
