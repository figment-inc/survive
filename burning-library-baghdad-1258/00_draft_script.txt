## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: The largest library in the world holds every book the Islamic Golden Age ever produced. The Mongols throw them in the river. You would not want to be in Baghdad, 1258.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: The Caliph refused Hulagu Khan's demand to surrender. Now a hundred and fifty thousand Mongols surround your city — and your walls were never built for a siege.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: Baghdad falls in thirteen days. The Mongols don't burn the books. They dump them into the Tigris — hundreds of thousands of manuscripts on medicine, astronomy, mathematics.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: The river runs black with ink. You watch centuries of knowledge dissolve downstream.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: One man's refusal to negotiate erased four hundred years of human discovery — in a single week.
