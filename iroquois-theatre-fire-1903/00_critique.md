### Closure — 7/10

**Failing line (Clip 05):** "You couldn't open a single door — every one bolted shut to protect ticket revenue inside a building they called fireproof. Six hundred and two dead."

**Problem:** The death toll as the final words signals summary. The irony of "protect ticket revenue / fireproof" is the actual revelation — it should be the last thing ringing in the viewer's ears, not the number. Also "You couldn't open a single door" slightly restates what Clip 04 already showed.

**Fix:** "Every door bolted shut to protect ticket revenue — inside a building they called fireproof. Six hundred and two people never reached the street."

This embeds scale inside a concrete image rather than ending on a bare number, and the "fireproof" irony lands as the conceptual punchline even though it's mid-sentence — the viewer is still processing it when "never reached the street" cuts to black.
