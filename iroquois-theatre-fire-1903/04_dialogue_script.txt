## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Chicago builds a theatre and calls it absolutely fireproof. Thirty days later, you would not want to be inside the Iroquois Theatre, 1903.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: It's a Wednesday matinee — balconies packed with women and children. You notice the exit doors have no handles on the inside. Management bolted them shut to stop people sneaking in free.

## Clip 03 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: You run. The doors don't give. They open inward. Bodies stack ten deep against locked exits.

## Clip 04 — Revelation
NARRATOR: You couldn't open a single door — every one bolted shut to protect ticket revenue inside a building they called fireproof. Six hundred and two dead.
