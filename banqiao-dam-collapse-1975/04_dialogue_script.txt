## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Typhoon Nina drops a year's worth of rain in six hours. Your dam's sluice gates are clogged with sediment. You would not want to be below Banqiao Dam, 1975.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: You radio for the upstream floodgates to open. The request is denied — authorities won't risk flooding the harvest downstream.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: The water rises past the dam's maximum. You feel the concrete shudder beneath your feet as cracks split the face. Sixty-one dams upstream are already failing.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: They saved the harvest and lost two hundred thousand people.
