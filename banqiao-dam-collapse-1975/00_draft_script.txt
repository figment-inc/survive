## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Typhoon Nina drops a year's worth of rain in six hours. The sluice gates on your dam are clogged with sediment — they can't open. You would not want to be below Banqiao Dam, 1975.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: Engineers radio for the floodgates to be opened upstream. The request is denied — authorities don't want to flood the harvest downstream.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: The water rises past the dam's maximum. You watch cracks spread across the face. Sixty-one other dams upstream are failing too.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: Banqiao collapses at one in the morning. A ten-metre wall of water moves faster than you can run — for fifty kilometres.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: They could have opened the gates and flooded the crops. Instead they saved the harvest and lost two hundred thousand people.
