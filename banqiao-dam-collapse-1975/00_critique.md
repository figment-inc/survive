## VERDICT: PUBLISH

This is one of the strongest scripts I've seen. The causal chain is airtight, the human decision is visible and damning, the turn at sixty-one dams genuinely shocks, and the ending is a masterclass in closure-as-revelation. One note below.

## LINE-LEVEL CRITIQUE

### You-As-Subject — 7/10

**Failing line (Clip 04):** "You're asleep when Banqiao collapses at one a.m."
**Problem:** "You" is grammatically present but entirely passive — asleep, unconscious, unable to act. The catastrophe happens TO the landscape while "you" is inert. This is the climax; "you" should be suffering or failing to escape, not sleeping through the setup.
**Fix:** "Banqiao collapses at one a.m. You wake to a sound like artillery. A ten-metre wall of water moves faster than you can run." — Now "you" wakes, hears, and fails to escape. Active verbs in the moment of catastrophe.

**Failing line (Clip 05):** "They saved the harvest and lost two hundred thousand people."
**Problem:** "They" as subject in the final clip. "You" disappears entirely at the moment of greatest impact. The ending is so strong on irony that it earns its place, but the POV shift is a minor crack.
**Note:** I would NOT change this line. The shift to "they" is what makes the irony land — it pulls the camera back to indict the decision-makers. This is one of those rare cases where breaking the "you" convention serves the script. Flagging it for transparency, not as a required fix.
