## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Scientists say sharks can't bite through human bone. Then five people are bitten in twelve days along the Jersey Shore. You would not want to be in New Jersey, 1916.

## Clip 02 — Escalation
NARRATOR: You live eleven miles inland on a tidal creek. A juvenile great white follows the current upstream — somewhere every expert said it couldn't survive. A dorsal fin appears.

## Clip 03 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: Something brushes your leg. The creek is sixteen feet wide. Both banks are right there.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: Four dead, one shark, eleven miles from the ocean — and every beach in America closed the next morning.
