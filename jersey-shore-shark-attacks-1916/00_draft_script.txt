## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Nobody believes sharks attack humans in shallow water. Then five people are hit in twelve days along the Jersey Shore. You would not want to be in New Jersey, 1916.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: Scientists insist a shark's jaw is too weak to bite through human bone. Beaches stay open. No warnings are posted.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: A shark swims eleven miles up a freshwater creek — somewhere experts said a saltwater predator couldn't go. It attacks three people in thirty minutes.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: You're swimming in knee-deep water. The creek is sixteen feet wide. There is nowhere to go.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: Every expert said it was impossible — wrong species, wrong water, wrong depth. The shark didn't read their papers.
