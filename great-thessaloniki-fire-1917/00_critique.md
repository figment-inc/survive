## LINE-LEVEL CRITIQUE

### Gut Punch — 7/10

**Failing line (Clip 04):** "Heat hits you from two streets away. You grab what you can carry. Your home folds into itself behind you."

**Problem:** This is good — "folds into itself" is a strong image. But Clip 04 is the catastrophe moment and it reads slightly cinematic rather than sensory. "Heat hits you from two streets away" tells the viewer about heat but doesn't make them feel it. And "grab what you can carry" is a common disaster phrase that doesn't cut deep. The script earns its understanding brilliantly but leaves a half-step of visceral potential on the table.

**Fix:** Push the sensory texture one notch harder. Something like: "The air sears your lungs two streets away. You grab a blanket, a photograph — your roof is already sagging. Your home folds into itself before you reach the corner." The lung detail puts the viewer in a body. The specific objects (blanket, photograph) make the loss concrete. The time pressure ("before you reach the corner") tightens the trap.

This script is ready. The only real upgrade path is making the catastrophe clip more physically uncomfortable. Everything else — structure, causality, closure, restraint — is near gold standard.
