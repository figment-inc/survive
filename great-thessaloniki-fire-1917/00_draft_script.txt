## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: A kitchen fire starts in a refugee shack. The city has no running water — the Ottoman pipes haven't worked in years. You would not want to be in Thessaloniki, 1917.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: Seventy thousand refugees are packed into wooden houses with shared walls. Wind carries embers across narrow streets like a fuse.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: Firefighters find the hydrants dry. They try to dynamite buildings to create a firebreak — but the houses are too close together.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: The fire burns for thirty-two hours. You watch your entire neighborhood disappear block by block.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: The pipes could have been repaired any year for a century. Nobody paid for it. Seventy-two thousand people lost everything they owned.
