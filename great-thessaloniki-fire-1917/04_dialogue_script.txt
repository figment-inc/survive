## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: A kitchen fire starts in a refugee shack. The city has no running water — the pipes haven't worked in decades. You would not want to be in Thessaloniki, 1917.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: You share a wooden wall with six families. Wind pushes embers across your street before you can stamp them out.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: You crank open a hydrant — nothing. Firefighters dynamite a row of houses to build a gap, but the blast scatters burning timber across the rooftops behind you.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: Those pipes could have been fixed any year for a century. Nobody paid — and seventy-two thousand people walked out with nothing but what they carried.
