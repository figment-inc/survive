## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: A warship carries enough gunpowder to level a castle. Its gun ports sit barely a meter above the waterline. You would not want to be aboard the Vasa, 1628.

## Clip 02 — Escalation
NARRATOR: You sail out of Stockholm harbor. A gust hits the sails. The ship heels — water floods through the open gun ports.

## Clip 03 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: You feel the deck tilt past recovery. Cannons slide. The sea pours in. You have minutes.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: A king's vanity put sixty-four cannons on a ship that couldn't carry them. Thirty people drowned in sight of the crowd that came to cheer.
