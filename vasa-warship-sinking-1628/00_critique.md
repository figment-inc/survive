**Problem:** Final clip drops "you" completely. The viewer is ejected from the experience at the moment it should hit hardest.

### Escalation Curve — 7/10

**Fix:** Add a "trap" beat — the gun ports are locked open for the maiden voyage salute. The escape route (closing ports) is already gone before the gust hits. **Fix:** "The deck tilts past recovery. Cannons tear free of their mounts. Seawater reaches your chest before you reach the hatch." — Now the viewer is trapped, racing, losing.
