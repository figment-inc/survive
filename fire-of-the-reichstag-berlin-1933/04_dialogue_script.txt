## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Germany's parliament building is burning — and the government already has a plan. You would not want to be in Berlin, 1933.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: A lone arsonist sets fire to the Reichstag. He's caught inside. But you don't get an investigation — you get an excuse.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: By morning, your phone is tapped. Your newspaper is gone. Your neighbor disappears — no charge, no trial. President Hindenburg signs an emergency decree, and every civil right in the constitution is gone.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: The decree was drafted before the ashes cooled. One fire gave Hitler the power he never gave back.
