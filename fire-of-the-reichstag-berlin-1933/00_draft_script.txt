## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Germany's parliament building is burning — and the government is glad. You would not want to be in Berlin, 1933.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: A lone arsonist sets fire to the Reichstag. He's caught inside. But Hitler doesn't want an investigation — he wants an excuse.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: By morning, emergency powers suspend every civil right in Germany. No free press, no privacy, no trial before prison.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: You didn't set the fire. Doesn't matter. Your name is on a list. Thousands are arrested in days.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: The decree was drafted before the ashes cooled. One fire gave Hitler the power he never gave back.
