## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: January 1888 — it's sixty degrees on the Dakota prairie. Farmers send children to school in shirtsleeves. You would not want to be on the Great Plains today.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: By afternoon, a cold front drops temperatures forty degrees in minutes. A wall of snow hits — you cannot see your own hand.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: Your schoolteacher ropes thirty children together and walks toward town. Visibility: zero.

## Clip 04 — Aftermath
NARRATOR: Their parents dressed them for a spring morning. Two hundred thirty-five dead — mostly children, mostly found within two hundred yards of shelter they never saw.
