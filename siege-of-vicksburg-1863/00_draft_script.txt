## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: Grant's army can't break Vicksburg's defenses — so he decides to starve an entire city instead. You would not want to be in Vicksburg, 1863.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: Union guns surround you from land and river. No food, no supplies, no reinforcements. You dig caves into the hillside to survive the shelling.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: Weeks pass. You eat mules, then rats. The army bakes bread from ground corn cobs and wallpaper paste.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: Shells fall every hour. You can't sleep, can't surface, can't surrender — your commander refuses to negotiate.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: He finally surrendered on the Fourth of July. Vicksburg didn't celebrate that holiday again for eighty-one years.
