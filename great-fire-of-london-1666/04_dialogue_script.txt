## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: A spark lands on a baker's woodpile after midnight — the entire street is timber and pitch. You would not want to be in London, 1666.

## Clip 02 — Mechanism
NARRATOR: You grab a bucket, but the city has no fire brigade — just hand-pumps too slow to matter. The mayor refuses to demolish houses for firebreaks, afraid he'll pay for rebuilding.

## Clip 03 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: Four days. The fire jumps block by block. You watch thirteen thousand houses burn. Molten lead from St. Paul's roof pools at your feet.

## Clip 04 — Revelation
NARRATOR: You lost six hundred years of city because one man wouldn't tear down a row of houses.
