## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: The bow door on your ferry was designed for calm harbors — tonight you're crossing the Baltic in a storm. You would not want to be on the Estonia, 1994.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: To load cars quickly, the entire front of the ship opens like a mouth. One steel lock holds the sea out.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: The lock fails. Waves flood the car deck — hundreds of tonnes of water sliding side to side. The ship rolls thirty degrees in minutes.

## Clip 04 — Turn
NARRATOR: You climb toward the outer deck. The stairs are now walls. Most passengers are trapped in their cabins below the waterline.

## Clip 05 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: The Estonia goes under in thirty-five minutes. The water is six degrees. You have fifteen minutes before your muscles stop working.

## Clip 06 — Ending
NARRATOR: Engineers warned the bow door design was too weak for open sea. The ferries kept sailing anyway — 852 people were on board that night.
