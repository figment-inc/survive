## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: The bow door on your ferry was designed for calm harbors — tonight you're crossing the Baltic in a storm. You would not want to be on the Estonia, 1994.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: The entire front of the ship opens like a mouth to load cars. One steel lock holds the sea out.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: The lock fails. Hundreds of tonnes of water flood the car deck around you, sliding side to side. The ship rolls thirty degrees. You climb for the outer deck — the stairs are now walls.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: Engineers had warned the bow door was too weak for open sea. Eight hundred and fifty-two people were on board. The ferries kept sailing.
