## LINE-LEVEL CRITIQUE

### Narrator Restraint — 7/10

**Failing line (Clip 03):** "another minute and your belt armor covers you"
**Problem:** This explicitly tells the viewer that escape is one minute away before the shell hits — it's not quite foreshadowing the disaster, but it does signal "something is about to go wrong right now" by framing the near-miss so precisely. It's borderline acceptable because it's also educational (explaining the tactical situation), but it slightly reduces surprise.
**Fix:** Lean into the action rather than the countdown: "You're closing — belt armor almost covers you. A shell from Bismarck arcs over it and punches through the deck into the magazine." This removes the explicit "one more minute" countdown that signals the turn.
