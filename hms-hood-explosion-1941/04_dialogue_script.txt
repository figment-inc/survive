## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: You serve on HMS Hood — the largest warship in the world. Her deck armor is too thin for modern shells, but nobody upgrades her. You would not want to be on the Hood, 1941.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: You're sent to intercept the Bismarck in the Denmark Strait. Your captain closes fast — because at long range, plunging shells hit your weak deck.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: You're closing — another minute and your belt armor covers you. A shell from Bismarck arcs over it and punches through the deck beneath your feet into the magazine.

## Clip 04 — Ending
NARRATOR: Three men survive out of 1,415. The refit that would have saved you was postponed six times.
