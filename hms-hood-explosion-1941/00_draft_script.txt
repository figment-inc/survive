## Clip 01 — Hook
NARRATOR: HMS Hood is the largest warship in the world. Her deck armor is too thin for modern shells — but nobody upgrades her. You would not want to be on the Hood, 1941.

## Clip 02 — Setup
NARRATOR: You're sent to intercept the Bismarck in the Denmark Strait. Your captain closes the distance fast — because at long range, plunging shells hit your weak deck.

## Clip 03 — Escalation
NARRATOR: A shell from Bismarck arcs over your belt armor and punches straight down through the deck into the magazine.

## Clip 04 — Catastrophe
NARRATOR: The Hood breaks in half. Bow and stern rise like a closing book. She sinks in three minutes.

## Clip 05 — Ending
NARRATOR: 1,415 men aboard. Three survived. The refit that would have saved you was postponed six times.
